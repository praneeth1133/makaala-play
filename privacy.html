Makaala Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026 · Makaala Games · praneetharnepalli@gmail.com

Who we are

Makaala is a Mancala board game published by Makaala Games. This policy covers the Android, iOS and web versions.

What we collect

  • An anonymous player id created by Unity Authentication, used to run online matches, save progress and leaderboards.
  • Gameplay events (game start, moves, captures, game end, difficulty, screens viewed) and crash reports, with device model and OS version for diagnostics.
  • The display name and avatar you choose.
  • Advertising: the free version shows ads served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity Ads may use your device advertising identifier and IP-derived approximate location to serve and measure ads, subject to your device ad-tracking settings. No ads are shown after the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details.

We do not access contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Why

To run online games (Unity Relay and Lobby), sync progress (Cloud Save), keep leaderboards, fix crashes, understand which modes are played, and fund the free version with ads.

Processors

Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services, Unity Analytics, Unity Ads: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy), Google Play and Apple for purchases and notifications.

Retention

Analytics 13 months; cloud save until you delete it; crash logs 90 days.

Your controls and data deletion

To request deletion of your data: use "Delete my data" in Settings > Account inside the Makaala app, or e-mail praneetharnepalli@gmail.com with your player id (shown in Settings > About). Cloud save, leaderboard entries and analytics tied to your player id are deleted within 30 days; crash logs expire after 90 days. Analytics consent toggle in Settings. "Delete my data" in Settings > Account wipes cloud save and requests analytics deletion. Opt out of personalised ads through your device settings (Android: "Delete advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"; iOS: App Tracking Transparency). Local data is removed on uninstall. You can also email us to exercise GDPR, CCPA or DPDP (India) rights.

Children

Makaala is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security and transfers

Data travels over TLS and is stored in Unity data centres for the project's region.

Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes.
