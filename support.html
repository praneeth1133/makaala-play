How to play

Each player owns the six cups on their side and the store on their right. Tap one of your cups to pick up its seeds and sow them one by one, counter-clockwise, into the following cups and your own store. Land your last seed in your store to take another turn. Land it in an empty cup on your side to capture that seed and every seed in the opposite cup. The game ends when one side is empty, and the player with more seeds wins.
